This website, what happens when you visit: we use no first-party cookies and no analytics or advertising tools, and there are no server-side forms: messages go out from your own email app. Three things you should know.

1. Google Fonts. Typefaces load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and the usual technical request data, such as your browser. We do not receive that information.

2. Animation libraries. They load from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr, which also receive your IP address when serving them. We do not receive that data either. Planned: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses in its technical logs [unconfirmed until published].

3. Local storage (localStorage). Your browser keeps on your device only the demo style you picked, to remember it on your next visit. It is not personal data, is not sent to any server and cannot be used to track you. You can delete it in your browser settings.

Changes to this notice: any change will be posted on this page with its date.
